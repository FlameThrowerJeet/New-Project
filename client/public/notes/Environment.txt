	 	- Earth par, 3 different tareeko se Nitrogen Fixation hota hai : 
	 	
	 		1) Micro-organims se :  Bacteria and Blue-Green Algae  ---> Ye, Nitrogen ko Ammonium Ions me convert kar dete hain. 
	 		
	 			- Free living nitrifying bacteria : Aerobic Azotobacter   and    Anerobic Clostridium
	 			
	 			- Symbiotic nitrifying bacteria jo leguminous root nodule plants : Ex --> Rhizobium
	 			
	 			- Blue-Green Algae : Ex --> Anabaena, Spirulina

	 				- In nitrates ko plant log kheench lete hain soil se, and convert them into amino acids---> Building blocks of proteins. 
	 				
	 				- Soil nitrates, paani me highly soluble hote hain---> Transported away by surface or ground water. 
	 				
	 				- Soils or Oceans me -- Special denitrifying bacterias hote hain--> Pseudomonas ---> Ye, Nitrates/Nitrites ko elemental Nitrogen me convert kar dete hain.

	  Ecological Succession : Ecological time scale par, directional change in vegetation 
	  
	  	- Ye hota hai, jab a series of communities ek dusre ko replace kar deti hai, due to a large scale destruction - natural or man-made. 
	  	
	  	- Ye hota rehta hai, jabtak ek stable mature community na develop ho jaye. 
	  	
	  	- Ex ; Annual Plants ---> Perennial Plants and Grasses ----> Shrubs ----> Softwood Trees-Pines ----> Hardwood Trees
	  	
	  	- Pehla plant jo aata hai colonise karne kisi area ko ---> Pioneer community. 
	  	
	  	- Final stage of succession --> Climax community. 
	  	
	  	- Increased productivity  ,    Shift of nurtients from reservoir,   Increased organism diversity with Increased niche development,    Gradual increase in complexities of food webs. 
	  	
	  
	 	- Secondary succession shuru hota hia, well developed soil par, already formed at the site. ---> Relatively faster. 
	 	
	 
	 
	 Autogenic Succession : Jab, community ke living inhabitants ki wajah se succession aaye. 
	 
	 Allogenic Succession : Jab koi outside force ki wajah se aaye. 
	 
	 
	 Autotrophic Succession : In which, initially, the green plants are much greater in quantity. 
	 
	 Heterotrophic Succession : In which, heterotrophs are greater in quantity. 
	 
	 
	 	- Continets ke middle me situated areas me, Succession would occur faster.

	Acquatic Oganisms ko classify kiya jata hai---> On the basis of their zone of occurance  and their ability to cross these zones. 
	
		1) Neuston : 
		
			- Unattached organisms, jo air-water interface par rehte hain ---> Ex : Floating plants
			
			- Kuch organisms, jaise water strider, puri life, air-water interface ke upar hi rehte hain. 
			
			- Kuch organisms, jaise beetles and back-swimmers, iske neeche rehte hain. 
			
		
		2) Periphyton : 
		
			- Ye organisms, Rooted plants ki stems and leaves,  Ya fir ye, bottom mud se emerge hone wale mud se attached hokar rehte hain. 
			
			- Ex : Sessile Alage and their associated group of animals
			
		
		3) Plankton : Microscopic plants like algae(Phytoplankton)  and animals like crustaceans  and  protozoans(Zooplanktons), found in all aquatic ecosystems
		
			- Planktons jyada hil-dul nahi sakte, isliye inki movements, currents hi control karte hain
			
			
		4) Nekton : Wo animals, jo swimming karte hain. 
		
			- Ye relatively large  and  powerful hote hain, kyunki inhe water currents ko overcome karna hota hai 
			
			- Insects(2 mm long) se leke, Blue Whales, sab saaale Nekton hain
			
			
		5) Benthos : Water mass ke bottom par milne wale organisms
		
		
		
		
	Aquatic Habitats kai zones me distributed hota hai, depending upon the penetration of sunlight. 
	
		1) Photic Zone : Upper layer of aquatic ecosystems ---> Jaha light penetrate kar jaati hai ---> Saara photosynthesis, yahi tak confined hota hai. 
		
			- Is zone ki depth, pani ki transparency par depend karti hai. 
			
			- Ya photosynthesis or respiration, dono hota hai. 
			
			- Ise euphotic zone bhi kehte hain ===> Lighted and well-mixed portion, that extends from the lake surface down to where the light level is 1% of that at the surface. 
			
		
		2) Aphotic Zone : Aquatic Ecosystems ki lower layers, jaha light penetration and plant growth restricted hota hai
		
			- Yaha bas respiration hota hai. 
			
			- Ye, littoral and Photic zone ke neeche hota hai, jaha light levels is too low for phtosynthesis. 
			
			- Deep unlit region ko, Profundal ZOne kehte hain.

		- Fresh water me,  iski concentration hoti hai, 0.0010 % by weight ----> Air se, 150 times kam. 
		
		- Aquatic ecosystem me air enter karti hai--> Through Air water interface and through photosynthesis. 
		
		- To quantity of DO depends upon these 2 activites. 
		
		- Temperatue : Water-Water me oxygen kam soluble hoti hai.   Warm-water me decomposer activity bhi badh jati hai. 
		
			- Isliye, temperature badhane se, Dissolved Oxygen kam ho jati hai. 
			
			- Jab DO, 3 to 5 ppm se kam ho jaye, tab bohot saare aquatic organisms dam tod dete hain. 
			
		- Transparency : Turbid water me light penetration nahi ho pata jis se photosyntheic activity kam ho jati hai.

	Critically Endangered (CR)
	
		1) Reduction in population (>90% over the last 10 years)
		
		2) Population size (< 50 mature individuals)
		
		3) Probability of extinctions in wild at least 50% in their last 10 years
		
		4) Facing extremely high risks of extinction in the wild
		
	
	Endangered (EN)
	
		1) Reduction in population size (70% over the last 10 years)
		
		2) 250 se kam mature individuals
		
		3) Probability of extinction in wild at least 20% within 20 years
		
		4) Facing very high risk of extinction in the wild
		
	
	Vulnerable (VU)
	
		1) Reduction in population (>50% in the last 10 years)
		
		2) 10,000 se kam mature individuals
		
		3) Probability of extinction in the wild at least 10% within 100 years
		
		4) Facing high risk of extinction in the wild

	India me milne wale Floral Groups : 
	
		1) Algae : Green-non-differentiated(into organs like roots, stem and leaf) plants ,  jinke pass cholorophyll hota hai. 
		
			- Ye pani, ya moist situations me ugte hain. 
			
			- Fresh-water algae, green ya blue-green hoti hai
			
			- Marine algae, Red ya Brown hoti hai. 
			
			- Ye autotrophic podhe hote hain, jo khudka khana manufacture kar sakte hain. 
			
			
		
		2) Fungi : Non-Green,  Non-differentiated plants,  Total absence of Chlorophyll
		
			- Dead, Rotten Organic matters, jaise ki saprophytes par grow karti hai
			
			- Or, live as parasites on other living bodies -- Hosts--- > Jaise ki main. 
			
			- Moulds  and  Mushrooms - Examples of Saprophytic Fungi
			
			- India me max diversity milti hai--> Western Ghats > Eastern Himalayas > Western Himalayas
			
		
		3) Bacteria : Non-Chlorophyllous micro-organisms ---> Lead saprophytic or parasitic existence
		
			- Jyadatar pathogenic hote hain
			
			- Saprophytic bacteria kaam ke hote hain ---> Soil borne and used in industries
			
			
		
		4) Lichen : Alga + Fungus
		
			- Greyish green plants, which grow on rocks, Tree-trunks and dead wood etcc
			
			- Algae, food manufacture karke, fungus ko deta hai. 
			
			- Fungus pani absorb and retain karta hai, to keep the algal cells moist 
			
			- Most common in wetlands,  Rare in rivers and streams,  not found in ground water
			
			
		
		5) Bryophytes : Plant is differentiated into a small stem and simple leaves, lekin, true roots absent hoti hai. 
		
			- Usually moist places me grow karte hain Ex : Liverworts, Mosses
			
			- India ke 2nd largest Green plants ka group hai.   
			
			- Mosses > Liverworts > Hornworts
			
		
		6) Pteridophytes : Well differentiated plant bodies,  consisting of roots, stems and leaves. ----> Posses vascular bundles
		
			- Jyadatar terrestrial hote hain----> Flourish well in moist and shady places ---> Kuch Aquatic hote hain. 
			
			- Vascular Cyptograms like, Club-mosses,  Horse-tails and ferns ---> Usually distributed all over the world
			
			- North-East > South-India > North-India
			
			
		
		7) Gymnosperms : 	Naked-Seeded plants
		
			- Simple Flowes without accessory whorld and Stamens and carpels--> Remain aggregated in cones. 
			
			- Carpels ke suface par ovules present hota hai, jo directly pollinate kiya jata hai, pollen grain se. 
			
			- Cycas,  Pinus,  Gnetum
			
		
		8) Angiosperms : Closed-Seeded plants
		
			- Sabse developed platns, bears flowers . 
			
			- Carpels have ovary, Style and Stigma
			
			- Ovary, usually fruits me develop hoti hai,  or ovules, seeds me. 
			
			- Isliye, seeds, fruits ke andar hi rehte hain. 
			
			- India ke pass, 7% of the world's flowering plants hain.

	Insan log jo, CO2 produce karte hain, uska 1/3rd, Oceans absorb kar lete hain. ---> Is wajah se oceans ka acidification hota hai.
	
		- CO2 ka uptake se, Hydrogen Ions ki concentration badh jati hai oceans me----> Carbonate ions ki concentration kam ho jati hai----> Ph of the oceans decreases--: Oceans become less alkaline. 
		
		- Abhi ocean ki Ph 8.0 hai---> It is alkaline. 
		
		- Industrial revolution ke baad se, Ph 0.1 kam ho gayi hai---> Hydrogen ions ki concentration 26% badh gayi hai. 
		
		- OCeans ki Ph 7 se kam hona to impossible sa kaam hai. 
		
		
	CO2 + H2O ---> H2CO3 (Carbonic Acid)  ---> Ye sala disintegrate ho jata hai, H+ ions and HCO3-   bicarbonate ions me.---> Ye further disintegrate ho jata hai, Carbonate and H+ ions me.  	
		
		- In Carbonate ions ka use karke, marine organisms, apne calcium carbonate shells and skeletons banate hain
		
		- Lekin, Ocean Acidification, ki wajah se, Carbonic acid and Bicarbonate ions, ki concentration badh jati hai, jis wajah se, Carbonate ions ki concentration kam ho jati hai. 
		
		
	Acidification ki wajah se, Coral Reefs grow nahi kar paate----> Less plankton production---> Less productive fisheries	
		
		
		
	Calcium carbonate ke 2 forms hoe hain " 
	
		1) Calcite : Mineral Form 	
		
			- Found in the shells of Planktonic algae,  Amoeboid Protists,  Some corals,  Echinoderms and some molluscs (Oyseters etc)
			
			- Ye kam soluble hota hai. 
			
	
		2) Argonite : More soluble form
		
			- Jyadatar corals, mollusks etc me yahi hota hai. 	
	
	
		
	
	- Deep, cold water me, Carbonate ions waise bhi kam hote hain---> Shells of most calcifying organims, dissolve
	
	- Surface waters, over saturated hote hain, carbonate ions se--->Do not dissolve these shells. 
	
	- Saturation Horizon : Ocean ka wo level, jiske neeche, Calcium carbonate minerals, dissolve ho jate hain. 
	
	- Ocean acidification ki wajah se, ye horizon ka level, upar se upar jata rahta hai----> Jyada se jyada calcifying organisms ki maa chudne lagti hai. 
	
	- Calcite walo ka horizon, jyada greater depth par hota hai, Aragonite walo se. 
	
	- Lysocline : The depth at which dissolution, strongly increases in deep ocean.

		3) Rill erosion : Jab sheet flows, kisi land surface par concentrate hona shuru ho jate hain---> Rill erosion hota hai. 
		
				- Leaves visible scouring on the landscape.
				
		4) Gully erosion : Rill wala evolve hoke, gully me convert ho jata hai---- As duration and intensity of rain continues to increase and runoff volume continues to accelerate. 
		
			- Gully wo scoured out area hota hai, that is not crossable with tillage or grading equipment
